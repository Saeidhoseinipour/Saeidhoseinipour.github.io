About

I am interested in statistical machine learning, co-clustering, latent variable models, and matrix factorization methods. This website presents selected research outputs, publications, and related software implementations.

Research Interests

My research focuses on co-clustering, latent block models, exponential family models, sparsity, non-negative matrix tri-factorization, and machine learning methods for structured data.
