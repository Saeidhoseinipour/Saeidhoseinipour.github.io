About

I earned my BSc in Statistics and Applications at IKIU (2009–2014), where I first got hooked on the idea that data can tell real stories. Then I moved to the University of Tehran for my MSc in Mathematical Statistics (2014–2016), diving deep into Bayesian inference and ranked set sampling. My PhD in Data Science at Amirkabir University of Technology (2016–2023) was where everything came together — I worked on latent block models and non-negative matrix tri-factorization for text co-clustering, guided by three great supervisors.

Research Interests

My research focuses on co-clustering, latent block models, non-negative matrix tri-factorization, large language models, and machine learning methods for unstructured data.
