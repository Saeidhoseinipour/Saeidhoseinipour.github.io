About

I am Saeid Hoseinipour. My research focuses on co-clustering, machine learning, latent block models, sparse exponential family models, non-negative matrix tri-factorization, and text mining.

This website is the official academic page for my research publications and related software repositories.

GitHub: https://github.com/Saeidhoseinipour
